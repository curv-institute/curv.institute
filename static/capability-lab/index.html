01 / THE RESEARCH THESIS

Optimization should follow capability, not just compression error.

A low-rank binary factorization may preserve most numerical structure while disrupting exactly the operations a deployed reader needs. RepSci supplies the language to describe that distinction.

03 / RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

One system. Distinct responsibilities.

Use the existing CURV work rather than building another quantizer, another readout theory, or another certification envelope.

DISCOVERY / WHAT TO PROTECTRIQ + CGC + TAQ

Identify component-level vulnerability, activation signals, feature density, and task sensitivity. Compare predictors instead of assuming one wins.

CONSTRAINTS / WHAT MUST HOLDMLR + Harmonizer

Specify task-critical invariants, allowed transformations, distribution shifts and resource ceilings.

TRANSFORMATION / WHAT TO COMPRESSLittleBit / LittleBit-2

Generate geometry- and rank-controlled binary factorization candidates with matched storage and optimization budgets.

↓   EXPERIMENTS + INDEPENDENT MEASUREMENT   ↓
OBSERVABILITYPoR readout ladder

Measure frozen and refit performance with explicit legal reader families, costs and held-out tasks.

STRUCTURAL VALIDITYRP-2 + finite interfaces

Search for hidden path dependence, nonlinear interactions, and counterexamples to coarse scorecards.

RESEARCH CONTROLRepSci / CurvOS

Freeze baselines, separate evidence tiers, enforce admissibility and preserve negative results.

FINAL DISPOSITIONRepSci Certify

Check native evidence against protected predicates, typed Pareto accounting, scoped claims and provenance. No certification from synthetic or unverified runs.

Implementation boundary: LittleBit source is CC BY-NC 4.0. Commercial use requires a separate licensing assessment or an independent compliant backend.

07 / DECISION GATES

No promotion without evidence.

Each gate has a specific reason to stop. Green synthetic tests never silently promote a real-model or deployment claim.

PROPOSED RELEASE CRITERIA
ORIGINAL STUDY G0—G5
Pass: pin upstream/model/tokenizer, hash all datasets, establish legal usage, verify healthy execution host, measure storage feasibility, and confirm nontrivial FP16 protected-task reference. Stop: any unverifiable provenance, unavailable checkpoint or unsafe GPU state.
Pass: exact witnesses independently recomputed, assumptions and counterexamples recorded, proposed new statements distinguished from established Blackwell/Le Cam results. Existing illustrative finite witness is not a full gate pass.
Pass: run LittleBit A/B end-to-end with the pinned `--use_itq` switch, matched training settings, verified effective rank, packed bytes and representative numerical tolerances. Stop: silent target-BPW inflation or unaccounted costs.
Pass: protected-task references above trivial floors; independent held-out component and task splits; frozen/refit distinction; a meaningful predictive or diagnostic signal beyond Wanda, TAQ-like sensitivity, reconstruction and position. Stop: readout signals are unstable or uninformative.
Pass to replication: favorable paired E–C held-out performance at identical actual bytes and appropriately accounted compute, with no unacceptable general-language regression. Practical exploratory target: +2 percentage points absolute, subject to preregistered analysis. Stop: wins vanish after cost matching.
Pass: another capable model family, at least two storage budgets, new seeds, end-to-end throughput and memory, independent environment rebuild, archived negative results and external review.
